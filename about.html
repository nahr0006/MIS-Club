About the MIS & BA Club

Empowering students to bridge the gap between technology and business

The Management Information Systems & Business Analytics (MISBA) Club at UMD Duluth brings together students interested in information systems, data analytics, and business technology. We run workshops, host guest speakers from industry, and collaborate on projects that help members build practical skills and professional networks.

Who should join?

  • Students majoring in MIS, Business Analytics, or related fields
  • Students interested in data, systems, and technology careers
  • Anyone looking to grow their professional network and portfolio

What members gain

  • Hands-on project experience for resumes and interviews
  • Access to guest speakers, alumni, and recruiters
  • Workshops on tools like Excel, SQL, Python, and visualization
